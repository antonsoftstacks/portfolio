04 — About

PORTRAIT

Anton Sigheartau

I was born in Romania and moved to the UK in 2013, aged eight, going on to primary school, secondary school and college here.

It was at college that I realised I wanted a career in creative media. I took a Level 3 BTEC in Creative Media (Moving Image) and spent two years on camera work, editing in Premiere Pro, After Effects and Photoshop, and documenting the progress of every project on websites I built in Wix.

That led me to the University of Staffordshire, where I'm now in the final year of a BA (Hons) in Creative Media Production. Along the way I've written scripts, edited in Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, shot on everything from a Canon 600D to a Sony FS5, and brought my graphic design to life with animation.
